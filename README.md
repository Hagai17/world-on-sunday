One image that sums up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026, drawn as the front page of a broadsheet newspaper, "The World on Sunday". Its eight panels carry the day's biggest stories: the COP31 climate talks in Antalya collapse without a deal; Iran strikes Bahrain as tankers are hit near the Strait of Hormuz; Russian strikes batter Ukraine, flattening a Kyiv apartment block; Mexico captures the FBI most-wanted cartel boss 'El Chapo Isidro'; an AI sell-off sends world stocks into correction; the World Court rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya; the FBI probes a hack of its national crime database; and ESA and JAXA's BepiColombo reaches Mercury after an eight-year journey. The program validates the picture against the competition gate (a whole PNG or JPEG, at most 1 MiB and 1024x1024, with chunk CRCs and segment structure checked) and copies it byte for byte to /output/entry.png.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
